Week 8

Tuesday · Presentation day

✦ Feedback and takeaway

This week we presented our DigiPhant project. My biggest takeaway was the feedback from our instructor: to think about ways that make it easier for the camera to recognize us, and to make the interaction itself simpler.

✦ Reflection

  • Most of our problems came from recognition, not from the elephant: overlapping bodies, lost performers, and winks that needed careful thresholds.
  • Splitting the screen was the biggest improvement so far, because it made the camera's job simpler instead of making the model smarter.
  • Next, I want to keep following that direction: fewer and bolder gestures, clearer positions for each performer, and actions that are easy to trigger and hard to trigger by accident.

Thursday · Guest lecture

✦ Takeaway

My biggest takeaway from the guest lecture was that design ideation and iteration can't rely on AI.

✦ Reflection

  • When I iterate from now on, I want to use more physical prototypes, instead of asking AI to make things for me.
  • I also saw the work of the physical groups. I'm really looking forward to working with them, but I'm also worried about how to blend the physical and the digital well.
